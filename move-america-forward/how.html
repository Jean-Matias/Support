How we’re doing it

We’re starting with two things we can actually build: a regular newsletter and local conversations where supporters can meet and decide what to work on together.

Where we start

  1. Publish a monthly update. Share what’s underway, which volunteer tasks are open, and what’s coming next.
  2. Try one local meetup. Start with one volunteer host and a small group. Use what participants learn to decide whether to organize another.
  3. Report back. Publish a short update on what happened, how donations were used, and what the next step would cost.

What your donation would support

Our first priorities are website hosting and newsletter tools. As local meetups take shape, donations could also help cover printing and basic event supplies.
